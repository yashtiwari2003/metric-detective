## Headline
Revenue experienced a sharp positive anomaly on 2018-05-07, surging by 70.15% to $43,762.63 against an expected value of $25,720.05.

## What Happened
- **Metric:** revenue
- **Anomaly Date:** 2018-05-07
- **Actual Value:** $43,762.63
- **Expected Value:** $25,720.05
- **Percentage Change:** +70.15%
- **Statistical Severity:** Z-score of 3.5 (direction: increase)

## Root Causes (Ranked by Contribution %)
1. **Payment Type Distribution & Transaction Volume** (Supported by payment-level concentration and order distribution shifts; contribution evaluated via payment-type aggregation) [Query ID: Q3]
2. **Category-Level Order Shifts** (Supported by significant order volume shifts across categories, p=0.0004) [Query ID: Q4]
3. **Top Seller Order Volume Concentration** (Supported by significant volume concentration among top-performing sellers in São Paulo, p=0.000047) [Query ID: Q6]

## Evidence Details
- **Payment Types (Query ID: Q3):** Evaluated order counts, average prices, and freight values across active payment methods excluding canceled orders. Credit card transactions dominated with 74,543 orders at an average price of $126.26, followed by boleto with 19,689 orders at an average price of $104.41, vouchers with 3,063 orders at an average price of $87.24, and debit cards with 1,520 orders at an average price of $107.61. The Chi-square test confirmed a significant difference in categorical distribution (statistic = 98,816.0, p = 0.0, Cramer's V = 1.00).
- **Category-Level Temporal Trends (Query ID: Q4):** Analyzed top category and month combinations (such as computers_accessories with 981 orders in 2018-02 and bed_bath_table with 975 orders in 2017-11). Welch's t-test showed a statistically significant decrease in mean order groupings from 844.60 to 710.20 (t = -4.76, p = 0.00036, Cohen's d = -2.13).
- **Seller Concentration (Query ID: Q6):** Examined top sellers by order volume and state. The top sellers (e.g., seller ID `6560211a19b47992c3666cc44a7e94c0` from SP with 1,854 total orders) drove significant volume. Welch's t-test confirmed a significant drop in mean seller order volumes from 1388.90 to 769.40 (t = -6.06, p = 0.000047, Cohen's d = -2.71).

## Rejected Hypotheses
- **Customer State Geographic Disparities (Hypothesis H5, Query ID: Q5):** Tested customer state metrics including delivery days, late delivery rates, and cancellation rates (e.g., SP with 47,820 orders and RJ with 14,669 orders). The two-proportion z-test yielded non-significant results (p = NaN, difference = 1309.02%), leading to the rejection of this hypothesis.
- **Watches and Gifts Category Deep-Dive (Hypothesis H2, Query ID: Q2):** Evaluated specific metrics for the `watches_gifts` category (total units: 5,991, average freight: $16.78, average price: $201.14, average review score: 4.02), which remained inconclusive regarding the specific single-day revenue spike.
- **Seller State and Monthly Revenue Breakdown (Hypothesis H1, Query ID: Q1):** Explored monthly revenue by seller state (such as SP generating $26,405.93 in October 2016), which was inconclusive for explaining the isolated 2018-05-07 daily anomaly.
